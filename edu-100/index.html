Introduction to Zscaler Zero Trust Exchange

8 labs (0–7) that take one Windows client from unprotected to Zero Trust: onboard a user, forward its traffic with Zscaler Client Connector, inspect and filter Internet access with ZIA, reach a private app through ZPA, and monitor a SaaS app with ZDX.

ⓘ Zero Trust Exchange in one minute — key terms
Experience CenterThe unified Zscaler console at console.zscaler.com, where you manage ZIA, ZPA and ZDX. Its Search menu opens any feature.
Service Portal (Authentication Service)Formerly ZIdentity. Holds users and user groups, and the service entitlements that decide which Zscaler services each user may use.
Zscaler Client Connector (ZCC)The endpoint agent on the Corp Client PC. A forwarding profile tells it how to send traffic to ZIA and ZPA; an app profile decides who gets that forwarding profile.
ZIAZscaler Internet Access: inspects Internet-bound traffic, including SSL/TLS, and enforces policies such as URL Filtering.
ZPAZscaler Private Access: gives users access to specific private applications, never the network, through outbound-only App Connectors and default-deny access policies.
App ConnectorA VM next to your applications that dials out to ZPA, so the data center opens no inbound ports.
ZDXZscaler Digital Experience: Web and Cloud Path probes measure application performance and hop-by-hop network health from users' devices.
