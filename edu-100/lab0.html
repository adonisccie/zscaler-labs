Lab Description and Scenario

For these lab exercises, you work with a scenario in which your organization, Labtenant, has deployed the Zscaler Zero Trust Exchange to protect against Internet threats, control Internet access, and provide secure access to private resources. The organization is also focused on end-user experience and uses the Zero Trust Exchange to monitor and report on digital performance. You deploy the Zscaler Client Connector (ZCC) to end-user devices to control Internet access, provide access to private applications, and probe, benchmark, and measure the digital experience for each user in the organization.

To Support the Rollout, You Are Tasked To

  • Connect users to the Zero Trust Exchange by deploying ZCC to Windows client devices.
  • Configure SSL inspection.
  • Configure user access to both Internet and private applications.
  • Deploy Zscaler Digital Experience (ZDX) reporting and create an initial set of ZDX applications and probes.

Note: Throughout the lab guide, <Student FQDN> refers to the domain assigned to your simulated tenant (e.g. zs000834.labtenant.com). Save it in the ⚙ Lab Configuration panel above — all placeholders update automatically.

Task 0.2 Lab Topology & Lab Setup ▾

Throughout the labs, you work with Zscaler Zero Trust Exchange services (ZIdentity, ZIA, ZPA, and ZDX) in a simulated environment, and separately have read-only access to a real Zscaler tenant.

Lab topology — the Experience Center (ZIdentity) is the single console for ZIA, ZPA, and ZDX. The Zero Trust Exchange cloud brokers connectivity between users, the Internet, and the Corporate Datacenter's App Connector.

Simulated Lab Exercises

The simulated lab exercises mimic real-world Zscaler deployments, providing a safe and controlled environment to practice configuration and administration tasks. These exercises help you become familiar with Zscaler administration and configuration interfaces and reinforce the core concepts taught in the course. Tasks marked for the simulated environment are where you actually build configuration — departments, users, entitlements, policies, App Connectors, and App Segments.

Read-Only Zscaler Tenant Access

In addition to the simulated environment, you receive access to a read-only Zscaler tenant through Zscaler Solutions Demo Center (SDC) credentials. This environment presents Zscaler core features and dashboards in a realistic, production-like context:

  • Provides visibility into real-world dashboards and configurations, even when full administrative access is not available.
  • Complements the simulated lab exercises by exposing you to Zscaler's broader ecosystem and integrated services.
