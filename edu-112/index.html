EDU-112 · Hands-On Lab

Introduction to AI Security Platform

An intern's one prompt to a public AI tool becomes a full investigation — no malware, no stolen password, just a data-handling event that only shows up in prompts and responses. Trace it through AI Protect, AI Guard, and AI Red Teaming to see how Zscaler discovers, enforces, and hardens against AI risk.

ⓘ AI Security Platform in one minute — key terms
AI ProtectDiscovery & visibility: Public AI usage, App Inventory, and AI Assets (models, agents, workloads).
AI GuardReal-time enforcement: inspects prompts and responses, applies Detector-based policy, maps to compliance.
AI Red TeamingAdversarial testing: runs simulated attacks (probes) against AI assets and scores their resilience.
DetectorAn inspection rule (PII, Secrets, Toxicity, Prompt Injection, etc.) that drives an Allow/Block/Detect decision.
Health Score0–100 resilience score for a Red-Teamed AI asset; below 75 keeps it out of "Secured" status.
