Zscaler for Users — Administrator

17 hands-on labs covering ZIA policies, ZPA private access and ZDX digital experience monitoring, in a live CloudShare environment with your own tenant plus the Solutions Demo Center.

ⓘ Zscaler in one minute — key terms
ZTEZero Trust Exchange: Zscaler's cloud platform that brokers every user-to-app connection and applies security policy in between.
ZIAZscaler Internet Access: cloud secure web gateway and firewall. Inspects and enforces policy on all internet and SaaS traffic.
ZPAZscaler Private Access: zero-trust access to internal apps. Users connect to applications, never to the network.
ZDXZscaler Digital Experience: end-to-end performance monitoring for users, devices, networks and SaaS applications.
ZCCZscaler Client Connector: the endpoint agent that forwards traffic to ZIA/ZPA and runs ZDX probes.
Service PortalFormerly ZIdentity: Zscaler's identity layer for users, groups, departments, entitlements and admin/user authentication.
